Map Displays

Map displays, as you can see on the other screen, contain a listing of loci and their location on a given map. The loci are color-coordinated for your convenience:
Loci colored in red are genes.
Loci colored in brown are detected by SSR.
Loci colored in grey are detected by RAPD.
Loci colored in violet are detected by AFLP.
Loci colored in purple are other probed sites.
Loci colored in green are RFLP loci.
Loci colored in orange are gene candidates.
Loci colored in sky blue are QTLs.
Loci colored in blue are others (ones that do not match the other categories).

Also, the backbone status of each coordinate is indicated by the color. Coordinates in bold black are backbone coordinates, whereas coordinates in grey are not backbone coordinates. Ones in plain black are unspecified, meaning they may or may not be backbone coordinates.

You can also copy and paste the map coordinate listing into Microsoft Excel or the spreadsheet of your choice quite easily. Select the contents of the coordinate table with your mouse, copy the data, then paste directly into the spreadsheet. Modern spreadsheets will automatically lay out the data for you.
