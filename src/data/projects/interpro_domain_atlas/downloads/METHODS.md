# Methods

## Genome selection

48 REFERENCE-grade assemblies from three MaizeGDB collections — NAM Founders (27), HiLo (7),
Pan-Andropogoneae (14) — selected by requiring `REFERENCE` in the assembly name. Two of the 14
Pan-Andropogoneae entries (`Td-*-2.0b`) are alternate haplotype assemblies of the same individuals
as their `2.0a` counterparts, not subgenomes; they were processed but held out of the primary
matrix to avoid double-counting alleles, leaving **46 distinct genomes**.

Outgroups: Arabidopsis (TAIR10), rice (IRGSP-1.0), sorghum (NCBIv3), wheat (IWGSC), barley
(MorexV3), soybean (v2.1), all from Ensembl Plants release 63.

All staged files were checksum-verified against MaizeGDB's published manifests.

## Gene models — two arms

**Curated arm.** The reference annotation shipped with each assembly. Proteomes were reduced to
longest-protein-per-gene: 2,071,612 genes from 3,694,646 input sequences (isoform ratio 1.78), zero
dropped, asterisk stop characters stripped. Outgroups: 296,817 genes.

**Helixer arm.** Helixer 0.3.6 with the `land_plant_v0.3_a_0080` model on GPU (48 genomes,
~192 GPU-hours). Transcripts translated with `gffread -S -E`; Helixer emits one transcript per
gene, so no isoform selection was required.

Both arms are carried through every downstream step and are never pooled.

### Helixer QC

All 48 genomes were annotated under a single model checksum. Gene counts track curated
references across a >2x range (43k–88k genes). Locus-level concordance against curated B73:
76% of Helixer loci match a curated locus (reciprocal ≥50% overlap, same strand), and 82% of
curated loci are recovered. Exon structure is squarely in the maize range (5.7–5.8 exons/mRNA,
14–15% single-exon) with full UTR annotation.

One genome (`Zm-CML530-REFERENCE-HiLo-1.0`) failed initially: its published FASTA contains an
inline `# COMMENT: Generated by ALLMAP...` **inside a chromosome 10 sequence line**. This is a
defect in the distributed assembly, not a tool failure — it was the only affected genome of 48.
The comment was stripped (336 bases from chr10, all 533 sequences otherwise preserved) and the
genome re-annotated to 44,589 genes, within the normal range. **This is worth reporting to the
HiLo assembly maintainers**: any alphabet-validating tool will hit it.

## Domain annotation

InterProScan 5.78-109.0, 17 analyses: Pfam 38.2, PANTHER 19.0, CDD 3.21, Gene3D, SUPERFAMILY,
SMART, ProSiteProfiles, ProSitePatterns, PRINTS, NCBIfam, PIRSF, PIRSR, Hamap, SFLD, AntiFam,
Coils, MobiDBLite. Run as three independent campaigns (reference, Helixer, outgroup) totalling
2,371 shards and ~35,000 core-hours, with zero shard failures.

FunFam was excluded: it cost 2.5x the entire core analysis set while returning zero integrated
InterPro accessions.

SignalP, TMHMM and Phobius are license-restricted and absent from this build. Receptor-kinase
versus receptor-like-protein calls therefore rest on domain architecture without membrane-topology
evidence and are flagged lower-confidence.

### Why a uniform re-scan was necessary

MaizeGDB's existing per-genome `interproscan.tsv` files contain **only Pfam** hits, with signature
dates from mid-2020 (Pfam 33.1 / InterPro 79–81 era). Restricting our scan to Pfam for a
like-for-like comparison on 400 B73 proteins gives Jaccard 0.840, precision 0.888, recall 0.939,
with 324/400 proteins identical — good agreement, and the residual disagreement is version drift
(21 of their accessions are retired or merged out of Pfam 38.2). But the full 17-analysis set
yields **2.92x more domain assignments** (1,576 vs 540) and 1,038 vs 389 distinct accessions.
The existing files are therefore not comparable across genomes at modern depth, and must not be
pooled with these. See `provenance/maizegdb_concordance.tsv`.

## Counting

Counting unit is the **gene**: a gene contributes 1 to a class regardless of how many matching
domains it carries. Matrices are genome × InterPro-accession gene counts.

## Functional class ontology

36 classes across 8 groups, covering 1,418 InterPro accessions, built by matching InterPro
*descriptions* with curated include/exclude regex rather than a hardcoded accession list, so the
ontology can be regenerated when InterPro updates. Classes are non-exclusive by design.

Two exclusions were derived empirically after they produced visible artifacts:

- **DNA replication** excludes reverse transcriptase, viral and mitochondrial-plasmid polymerases,
  and bacterial-only DNA polymerase III/DnaG. Before this, one genome showed a spurious 4.3x
  outlier (795 vs a mean of 185) driven entirely by retrotransposon content. CV fell 0.587 → 0.078.
- **Helicase** excludes Helitron, transposon, retroelement, viral and UvrD-like helicases.

Other lexical false friends caught during curation: `IPR002715` ("Nascent polypeptide-associated
complex NAC domain") is unrelated to NAC transcription factors; the bare word "kinase" otherwise
captures phosphofructokinase, pyruvate kinase and all phosphatases. Every rule and its rationale is
recorded in `ontology/maize_functional_classes.json`.

## Immunity classification

`ontology/maize_immunity_classes.json` assigns each gene to exactly one class by domain-architecture
precedence (NLR > NLR_partial > RLK > RLP > PR > IMMUNE_SIGNALING > IMMUNE_OTHER), keying on full
domain combinations rather than single domains. NLRs are further subclassed by N-terminal
architecture (TNL / CNL / RNL / NL / N_only / NLR_ID).

### External validation

Two independent checks the classifier was not tuned to pass:

1. **TIR-NLRs are 47.9% of dicot NLRs (median) but under 1% in every grass taxon** — recovering the
   known loss of TIR-NLRs in the monocot lineage.
2. **Arabidopsis resolves to 167 NLRs**, matching its published ~150–170 complement.

## Technical domain filter

Organellar and transposon-derived domains are flagged (`technical_flag`) in
`data/domain_status/*.tsv` and excluded from variance rankings by default, because they track
plastid/mitochondrial insertion into nuclear assemblies and transposon annotation depth rather than
gene copy number. They are **retained in the data**, never deleted.

Separately verified: the residual high-variance signal is not an artifact of annotation
completeness — of the top 40 CV domains, none correlates at r>0.7 with per-genome gene count.

## Reproducing

`scripts/` contains the full pipeline. Order:
`make_longest_protein.py` → `interproscan_array.sh` (or `helixer_array.sh` → `prep_helixer_protein.py`)
→ `aggregate_ips_arm.py` → `build_matrix.py` → `classify_classes.py`.
QC: `helixer_qc.py`, `compare_annotations.py`.
